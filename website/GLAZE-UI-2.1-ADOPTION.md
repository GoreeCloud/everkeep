The Continuity Center follows the Glaze UI 2.1 material rule **Content is solid. Interaction is glazed.** Durable recovery, continuity, assurance, boundary, and status content uses solid Surface material. Navigation and appropriate transient interaction may use controlled glaze.

The website carries the 48px general interaction floor, 56px Touch Assistance floor, density and clarity behavior, large-text fallback, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency handling, increased/forced-contrast resilience, safe-area behavior, and deterministic reduced-material behavior.

Glaze UI controls presentation only. It does not manufacture backup coverage, recoverability, restore validity, continuity readiness, failover authority, or production acceptance. A backup existing is not presented as equivalent to proven recoverability; recovery claims remain tied to current Everkeep evidence.

Glaze UI 2.0.0 remains the immediately preceding historical Stable baseline. Its adoption record may remain as historical documentation, but 1.x/2.0 assets are not part of the current 2.1 public build artifact.
