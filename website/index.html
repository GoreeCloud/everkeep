GoreeCloud Continuity Center

Preserve what must survive.

Everkeep is the substantive GoreeCloud platform system behind the Continuity Center. It governs resilience, backup and recovery, preservation, portability, continuity, succession, assurance, and digital legacy without turning an unsupported capability into a protection claim.

Explore EverkeepSource repository

Everkeep never turns an unsupported capability into a protection claim. Recovery and readiness state must be backed by current evidence. A backup existing is never presented as equivalent to proven recoverability.

Current state

Phase 3.5 continuity control-plane implementation is current.

Validated source now extends the original Everkeep core through Recovery Center 1.5, resource evidence timelines, recovery orchestration, restore-test scheduling, preservation and portability, succession, continuity objectives, recovery topology, failover planning and governance, a simulation-only controlled drill executor, rollback assurance, and separate failover operations and lifecycle projections. Everkeep also validates bounded GoreeCloud Mesh evidence-refresh intents and responses and only completes a refresh handoff when separately produced current Everkeep evidence is exactly bound to the intended authority, subject, assertion, producer revision, and response timing.

These capabilities remain evidence-scoped. The controlled executor and rollback path are simulation-only and do not authorize production failover, traffic switching, production data movement, production rollback, or source mutation. Runtime-capable Monitoring and Notify adapter boundaries exist in source, but source validation alone does not prove live ingestion or delivery. Production recovery effects and application-specific production acceptance remain separate evidence gates.
