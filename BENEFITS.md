# Everkeep — Benefits

Everkeep provides a shared continuity language so GoreeCloud components do not invent incompatible definitions of backup, restore, recoverability, preservation, portability, or succession.

Current source foundations provide these supportable benefits:

- **Truthful recovery state:** backup existence is kept separate from verified recoverability.
- **Fail-closed evidence:** missing, stale, malformed, unavailable, or unverified required evidence cannot silently become ready.
- **Source preservation:** recovery planning and current drill execution are designed to avoid mutating the source recovery point.
- **Portable recovery evidence:** contracts and manifests are designed to remain understandable across infrastructure changes.
- **Authority separation:** Identity, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Mesh, Policy, Observability, and application owners retain their own authority.
- **Measurable continuity:** RPO/RTO, evidence freshness, failure domains, topology, restore testing, and rollback can be represented explicitly.
- **Safer recovery development:** simulation-only execution and exact-plan governance support validation before consequential production authority is granted.

These benefits describe implemented Development capabilities and design properties. They do not claim production recoverability or Stable readiness for a workload that lacks accepted exact-environment evidence.
