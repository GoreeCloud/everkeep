# Everkeep — Competitive Objectives

Everkeep is intended to provide a GoreeCloud-native continuity and preservation control plane rather than duplicate a single backup product.

## Objectives

Everkeep should match or exceed mature continuity platforms in:

- evidence-backed backup and restore status;
- scheduled restore verification;
- recovery objectives and topology;
- dependency-aware recovery planning;
- preservation and portability;
- auditability and provenance;
- disaster-recovery planning;
- rollback assurance;
- privacy- and security-aware recovery gates;
- application-specific acceptance.

Everkeep should intentionally differ by:

- refusing to equate a successful backup job with recoverability;
- preserving separate Identity, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Policy, and Observability authority;
- treating evidence freshness and exact revision identity as first-class acceptance inputs;
- keeping portability, preservation, succession, and digital legacy alongside operational recovery;
- preferring open, self-hostable, replaceable foundations when practical.

## Current boundary

These are product objectives, not claims that every listed capability is deployed or production accepted. Verified current functionality is documented in `FEATURES.md`; planned work remains in `FEATURE-ROADMAP.md`.
