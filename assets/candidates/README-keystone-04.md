# Everkeep Keystone 04 — Refinement Review

Status: **non-canonical review candidate**.

`everkeep-keystone-04.svg` is a refinement of the preferred Keystone 03 direction. It does not replace the approved `assets/everkeep.svg` unless the GoreeCloud project owner explicitly approves it and the Everkeep visual identity contract is intentionally revised.

## Refinement goals

Keystone 04 concentrates the concept around three elements only:

1. **Preserved keystone** — the durable information, meaning, provenance, and recovery identity that must remain recognizable across change.
2. **Left continuity rail** — one independent path through infrastructure, storage, migration, or recovery change.
3. **Right continuity rail** — a second independent path, reinforcing continuity without implying a circular restore action or a single backup mechanism.

## Changes from Keystone 03

- Removed the secondary top and bottom connector strokes.
- Increased the two primary rail strokes from 5 to 6 units for stronger launcher and favicon recognition.
- Enlarged and simplified the central keystone geometry.
- Reduced the composition to a more stable three-part silhouette.
- Kept generous negative space between the preserved core and continuity rails.
- Preserved the teal-to-blue-to-violet Glaze UI gradient while ensuring the geometry does not depend on color to remain recognizable.
- Added `everkeep-keystone-04-monochrome.svg` as a geometry and accessibility validation form.

## Glaze UI alignment

The candidate uses restrained rounded terminals, simple optical symmetry, low visual noise, and a calm continuity metaphor. It intentionally avoids shields, locks, clouds, database cylinders, storage disks, circular restore arrows, recycle loops, and alarm-oriented security symbolism.

## Small-size criteria

Before promotion, verify the candidate at 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, and 512 px. At small sizes:

- the two rails must remain visually separate,
- the central keystone must remain the focal point,
- the interior record mark must not collapse into noise,
- no rail should appear to merge into the core,
- the mark must remain identifiable in monochrome,
- the silhouette must remain distinct from Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, GoreeCloud Backup, and generic restore icons.

## Candidate assets

## Promotion rule

Keystone 04 is not canonical or production artwork. Promotion requires explicit owner approval followed by intentional updates to:

- `assets/everkeep.svg`,
- `ICON.md`,
- the machine-readable Everkeep identity contract,
- any derived launcher, favicon, adaptive-icon, monochrome, and public presentation assets.
