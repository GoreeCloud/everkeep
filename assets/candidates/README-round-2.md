# Everkeep Identity Review — Round 2

Status: **non-canonical review candidates only**.

The previously proposed `everkeep-continuum-02.svg` direction was rejected by the GoreeCloud project owner on 2026-08-27 and is not part of this round.

The current approved `assets/everkeep.svg` remains authoritative unless and until one of these candidates is explicitly approved and the Everkeep identity contract is intentionally revised.

## Candidate 03 — Keystone

`everkeep-keystone-03.svg`

A fixed luminous keystone remains identifiable while independent continuity rails move around it. The direction emphasizes durable meaning and identity while infrastructure changes.

Review focus:
- strong small-size silhouette,
- no backup-arrow, cloud, database, shield, or recycle metaphor,
- clear distinction between the preserved core and the systems carrying it.

## Candidate 04 — Strata

`everkeep-strata-04.svg`

Three changing strata pass through one uninterrupted vertical core. The direction emphasizes information surviving multiple eras, storage layers, migrations, and implementation changes.

Review focus:
- layered-time metaphor without looking like storage media,
- recognizable central continuity element,
- calm Glaze UI geometry.

## Candidate 05 — Thread

`everkeep-thread-05.svg`

One continuous thread crosses three distinct milestones without breaking. The direction emphasizes succession, portability, recovery, and preserved meaning over time.

Review focus:
- continuity without forming a loop or restore-arrow metaphor,
- strong motion and memorability,
- suitability for launcher, favicon, monochrome, and adaptive-icon derivations.

## Promotion rule

No file in `assets/candidates/` is approved, canonical, public-production artwork, or an authority for derived assets. Promotion requires explicit owner approval followed by an intentional update to `assets/everkeep.svg`, `ICON.md`, and the machine-readable identity contract.
