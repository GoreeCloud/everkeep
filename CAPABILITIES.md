# Everkeep — Capabilities

## Overview

Everkeep is GoreeCloud's platform-wide continuity, recovery, preservation, portability, succession, digital-legacy, and recovery-assurance authority.

This capability record describes the current verified repository state. Everkeep is in **Development**. Source implementation, contracts, validators, simulations, drills, backup existence, and exact-revision tests do not by themselves establish production recoverability, production failover authority, release status, or Stable qualification.

## Core Capabilities

### Continuity and recovery evidence

Current Development source provides machine-readable foundations for:

- protected resources and protection policies;
- recovery points and verification state;
- evidence freshness and provenance;
- continuity status and acceptance;
- recovery objectives and topology;
- restore-test scheduling;
- live-acceptance evidence validation;
- recovery assurance and evidence timelines.

Everkeep distinguishes backup existence from verified recoverability. Missing, stale, malformed, unavailable, conflicting, or unverified required evidence must fail closed.

### Recovery planning and orchestration

Current source includes:

- dependency-aware recovery plans;
- isolated restore-verification contracts;
- source-preserving execution boundaries;
- controlled drill and simulation workflows;
- failover-plan governance;
- rollback planning and assurance;
- execution-time approval and evidence concepts.

Production-effect recovery, traffic switching, destructive restoration, and production failover remain separately gated and are not authorized by source-only implementation.

### Preservation, portability, and succession

Current source includes Development foundations for:

- preservation capsules;
- portability manifests;
- succession and digital-legacy eligibility;
- retention-aware continuity relationships;
- recovery metadata that can remain understandable across infrastructure changes.

These capabilities do not override Privacy Shield, Identity, Wardveil Security, application-owner, or legal/operational authorization requirements.

## User Capabilities

### Continuity Center

Everkeep includes a user-facing Continuity Center foundation for presenting continuity, recovery, topology, assurance, drill, rollback, and evidence-backed status.

The historical repository-specific presentation baseline is not current shared Glaze UI authority. Everkeep requires migration and consumer acceptance against current Stable **Glaze UI 1.6.0**.

### Recovery state communication

Everkeep is designed to distinguish states such as ready, attention, degraded, blocked, unknown, and not applicable based on current evidence rather than optimistic UI presentation.

Everkeep preserves Wardveil authority over security findings, integrity state, and recovery security gates. Live provider connectivity and production acceptance remain incomplete.

This repository implements Everkeep itself; a separate Everkeep-to-Everkeep integration is not applicable.

No accepted Observability runtime contract, continuity telemetry/health integration, freshness/completeness acceptance, or Everkeep-specific production evidence is established.

## Current Limitations
