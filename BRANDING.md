# Everkeep — Branding

## Identity

The official product name is **Everkeep**. Everkeep is the GoreeCloud platform identity for resilience, recovery, preservation, portability, continuity, succession, digital legacy, and recovery assurance.

The repository-synchronized canonical identity asset is `assets/everkeep.svg`. Branding authority is maintained by the canonical GoreeCloud branding repository, `GoreeCloud/branding-assets`.

## Usage rules

- Do not use generic placeholders in place of the approved Everkeep identity.
- Do not use Everkeep branding as evidence that a workload is backed up, recoverable, or production accepted.
- Do not replace the separate identities or authority of GoreeCloud Backup, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, GoreeCloud Identity, or other platform systems.
- User-facing Everkeep surfaces must follow the current applicable Stable Glaze UI contract.

The existence of an Everkeep logo or badge never upgrades missing, stale, or failed continuity evidence.
