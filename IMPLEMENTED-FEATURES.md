- continuity status, adoption, acceptance, and evidence contracts;
- protected-resource and protection-policy representation;
- recovery-point, verification, retention, and evidence services;
- PostgreSQL-oriented persistence and durable outbox semantics;
- dependency-aware recovery plans and source-preserving execution boundaries;
- Recovery Center evidence projections and resource evidence timelines;
- restore-test scheduling and isolated-restore verification contracts;
- preservation capsules and portability manifests;
- succession and digital-legacy eligibility contracts;
- RPO/RTO objectives, recovery topology, failure scenarios, and continuity posture;
- failover-plan approval and revision-bound acceptance evidence;
- simulation-only controlled failover and drill execution;
- rollback planning and rollback assurance;
- minimized GoreeCloud Monitor/Notify delivery adapters;
- authenticated GoreeCloud Mesh evidence-delivery source boundaries;
- fail-closed live-acceptance and evidence-freshness validation.
