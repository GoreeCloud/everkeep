# Everkeep — Privacy Policy

## Scope

Everkeep is a continuity and recovery authority. Its status, evidence, portability, preservation, and succession mechanisms must remain privacy-preserving and data-minimized.

This repository is in Development. Source contracts and tests do not by themselves establish a production data-processing practice.

## Privacy authority

GoreeCloud Privacy Shield remains authoritative for retention, deletion, export, transfer, succession, consent, purpose, and other privacy constraints. Everkeep must consume or respect that authority without minting or broadening it.

## Data minimization

Everkeep evidence should contain only information required to establish continuity state. Ordinary evidence, logs, dashboards, and status payloads must not contain reusable credentials, private keys, recovery codes, secret values, unrestricted backup contents, unnecessary personal records, or unrelated private content.

Where a digest, reference, bounded state, or provenance identifier is sufficient, Everkeep should not duplicate protected payloads.

## Recovery and preservation

Technical ability to restore data does not remove privacy, authorization, deletion, retention, or access-control requirements. Restore, preservation, portability, and succession actions must preserve applicable Privacy Shield, Identity, Wardveil Security, and application-owner boundaries.

## Sharing and external providers

Evidence transport through Mesh, Monitor, Notify, storage providers, or other infrastructure must be purpose-limited and minimized. A provider or adapter is not production accepted merely because source integration exists.

## Status presentation

Unknown, stale, missing, or failed privacy evidence must not be presented as a passing continuity state. Interfaces must communicate material limitations without exposing private recovery content.

This policy must be reconciled when verified implementation or accepted production behavior materially changes.
